# Cycle 002 — Concept Exploration

Prior art (cycle 1) not re-proposed: Orbit Duel, Gravity Flip Runner, Chrono Loop, Color Bind, Signal Weaver.

## Candidates
1. **Tether Swing** — one-button grapple; hold to attach a rope to the nearest anchor ahead, release to fling. Momentum-based endless course through procedurally placed anchors over a rising hazard. Feasibility 5, Novelty 4, Scope 5.
2. **Orbit Duel: Solo/AI** — evolution of cycle 1 with a bot opponent. Feasibility 4, Novelty 2 (iteration), Scope 4.
3. **Echo Bloom** — rhythm-ish survival where past positions leave damaging "echoes". Feasibility 4, Novelty 4, Scope 3 (tuning heavy).
4. **Tide Sorter** — water-level puzzle, pour/redirect fluid on a grid. Feasibility 3, Novelty 3, Scope 3.
5. **Lantern Drift** — stealth where light radius is also your health. Feasibility 3, Novelty 4, Scope 2 (AI + level design).

## Selection
**Tether Swing** (total 14): pure 2D physics, single-file HTML, no assets, one-input control that is testable headlessly.
