# Cycle 002 — Concept Exploration

Date: 2026-10-20
Phase: research
Prior art avoided: Orbit Duel, Gravity Flip Runner, Color Bind, Signal Weaver, Chrono Loop.

## Candidates
1. **Echo Cavern** — Single-player stealth/maze game in total darkness. Pressing Space emits an expanding sonar ring that briefly reveals walls and creatures it touches, but sonar also *attracts* blind hunters to the pulse origin. Reach the exit; fewer pings = higher score. Procedural DFS mazes (always solvable).
   Feasibility 5 / Novelty 4 / Scope 5 = 14
2. **Orbit Duel: Campaign** — Evolve cycle 1 with a bot opponent and waves. Feasibility 5 / Novelty 2 / Scope 4 = 11
3. **Tether Haul** — Two-body physics: swing a cargo pod on a tether around asteroids to deliver it. Feasibility 3 / Novelty 4 / Scope 3 = 10
4. **Rule Drift** — Arcade survival where the control mapping/rules mutate every 15s. Feasibility 4 / Novelty 4 / Scope 4 = 12
5. **Tide Farmer** — Cellular-automaton water-flow puzzle on a grid. Feasibility 3 / Novelty 3 / Scope 3 = 9

## Selection: Echo Cavern
Best total: pure canvas, no assets, procedural levels are solvable by construction, the
ping-vs-stealth tradeoff is a real design hook, and it fits one HTML file.
Runner-up: Rule Drift.
