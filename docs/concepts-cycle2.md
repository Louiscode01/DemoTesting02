# Cycle 002 — Concept Exploration

Date: 2026-11-10 | Phase: research

Prior art (cycle 1): Orbit Duel, Gravity Flip Runner, Chrono Loop, Color Bind, Signal Weaver. Not re-proposed verbatim.

## Candidates
1. **Tether Swing** — Single-player physics arcade. A ball swings from pegs on an elastic rope (hold to grapple the nearest peg in range, release to fling). Endless upward climb through procedurally placed pegs, rising hazard below. Feasibility 5, Novelty 4, Scope 5 (spider-swing / grapple genre, but minimal one-button take).
2. **Orbit Duel: Solo** — Evolution of cycle 1: AI opponent + waves. Feasibility 5, Novelty 2 (iteration), Scope 4.
3. **Lantern Drift** — Stealth puzzle with a limited-radius light that is also your health. Feasibility 4, Novelty 4, Scope 3 (level design heavy).
4. **Pulse Tiles** — Rhythm-grid puzzle where tiles activate on beat patterns. Feasibility 3 (audio sync), Novelty 3, Scope 3.
5. **Sand Scribe** — Draw terrain lines for falling sand to reach cups. Feasibility 3 (particle perf), Novelty 3, Scope 3.

## Selection: Tether Swing
Pure 2D physics, no assets, single HTML file, procedural content avoids level-design cost. Runner-up: Orbit Duel: Solo.
